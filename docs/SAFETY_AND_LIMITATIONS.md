# Safety, compatibility, and limitations

PromptMeld is experimental software with limited real-world testing. It depends
on Windows, the ChatGPT desktop app, Windows accessibility controls, and
third-party services whose behaviour and interfaces may change without notice.

## Compatibility limits

PromptMeld currently supports Windows 10 and 11 with the new ChatGPT desktop
experience. It does not currently automate the Classic ChatGPT desktop
experience or ChatGPT in a web browser.

Support for other ChatGPT experiences may be investigated if there is enough
user demand. Please describe your use case through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/an1uk/promptmeld-windows/issues).

## Review before use

PromptMeld may fail to capture, transform, paste, or submit text correctly.
ChatGPT may produce inaccurate, incomplete, inappropriate, or unexpected
content. Always review the completed prompt and resulting text before sending,
publishing, or relying on it.

Keep appropriate backups. Do not use PromptMeld or AI-generated output as a
substitute for professional advice or for safety-critical decisions.
